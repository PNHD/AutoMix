# Owner Blind Listening Instructions

Thank you for listening. This pack contains **11 short audio clips**,
grouped into 4 scenario sets. Each clip is roughly 25-30 seconds long and is
centered on a song-transition moment (part of one track, then a handoff into
another track).

## What to do

1. Listen to each clip with normal headphones/speakers, at a normal listening
   volume, in the order you like.
2. For **each clip**, fill in one entry in `OWNER_RATINGS_TEMPLATE.json`
   using the clip's exact filename (e.g. `S1-A`) as the key.
3. Please rate what you actually hear -- don't try to guess what
   technique/algorithm produced a clip. The filenames are intentionally
   opaque (`S1-A`, `S1-B`, ...) and carry no information about how the clip
   was made. There is nothing to identify -- just listen and rate.
4. It's fine (expected, even) if some clips within the same scenario set
   sound very similar to each other, or if one clip in a set sounds clearly
   worse. Rate each one independently and honestly.
5. When you're done, return the filled-in `OWNER_RATINGS_TEMPLATE.json` file.
   Please don't rename or reorder anything in it.

## Rating scale

For the six 1-5 ratings, use:

- **1** = very poor / clearly bad
- **2** = poor
- **3** = acceptable / neutral
- **4** = good
- **5** = excellent / exactly what you'd want

## What each rating means

- **seamlessness_continuity**: Does the handoff between the two songs feel
  like one continuous piece of music, or does it feel obviously stitched
  together?
- **timing_rhythmic_naturalness**: Does the beat/rhythm feel natural and
  steady through the transition, or does it feel rushed, dragging, or
  rhythmically "off"?
- **stretch_pitch_naturalness**: Does anything sound artificially
  sped-up/slowed-down, warbly, "chipmunk"-ish, metallic, or phasey?
  (If you don't notice anything like that, that's a good sign -- rate high.)
- **bass_low_end_transition**: Does the bass/low end handle the handoff
  cleanly, or does it feel muddy, doubled-up, or like it drops out
  awkwardly?
- **momentum_excitement**: Does the transition maintain or build energy
  appropriately, or does it feel like it kills the vibe?
- **overall_preference**: Your overall gut reaction to this specific clip.

## Yes/No questions

- **noticeable_hard_discontinuity**: Did you hear an obvious "click," pop,
  or hard cut/glitch at any point?
- **annoying_or_fatiguing_artifact**: Was there anything that would bother
  you if you heard it while just listening to music normally (not
  critically analyzing it)?
- **acceptable_for_normal_automix**: Would you be OK with this transition
  happening automatically while you're just listening to your music, without
  you having chosen it?

## Optional note

Free text -- anything you noticed, in your own words. Optional.

## One more thing

Please listen to **all clips and submit your ratings before** looking at any
other file, spreadsheet, or key that might explain what each clip is. The
point of this exercise is your honest, blind first impression.

Thank you!
